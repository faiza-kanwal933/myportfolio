About Me

Mathematics, taught with patience and structure

"My goal is simple — build a classroom where every student feels supported and capable of growth."

Faiza Kanwal is a Mathematics educator holding an MS in Mathematics (Completed — Result Awaited) from the University of Education, Lahore — Attock Campus, along with a B.Ed. (Secondary) and a BS in Mathematics. She is currently teaching Classes 9 and 10 at STEM School and College, Attock Cantt, and previously taught as a Junior Teacher at Army Public School, Attock Cantt, building on classroom experience first gained during an internship at FG Public School, Sanjwal Cantt.

She pairs a strong academic record with modern digital tools — LaTeX and Overleaf for clean technical documentation, Excel and Google Workspace for organisation, and AI-assisted tools for lesson preparation — to keep her teaching structured, current, and easy to follow.
